

# G+ Social Media Button Hider
<img width="338" height="769" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0070e0af-9550-4497-b582-0b078631aa76" />

A simple Tampermonkey script that hides annoying buttons and metrics on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and X (Twitter) so you can actually just scroll in peace.

There's a floating toggle menu in the bottom-right corner of your screen where you can turn things on and off without even refreshing the page.


## What it hides
<img width="672" height="690" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ad30b88f-a295-4b1e-995e-e42fa38c7f97" />

### Instagram
- **Likes & Counts:** Hides the heart button and like counts on posts and reels.
- **Reposts:** Hides the repost button (keeps the normal share button).
- **Saves:** Hides the bookmark button.
- **Comments:** Nukes the comment bubble, inline replies, and the "Add a comment" box entirely.
<img width="764" height="699" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb697697-73fe-4af2-98fb-6deca299a2ff" />

### Facebook
- **Like Buttons:** Hides the big "Like" button under posts.
- **Reaction Counts:** Hides the numbers showing how many people liked a post.
- **Comments:** Hides comment sections and buttons.
<img width="612" height="871" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b02d4b29-4915-4978-8e9d-82743d64908e" />

### TikTok
- **Likes & Counts:** Hides the heart button and the number next to it.
- **Comments:** Hides the comment button and the whole comment sidebar.
<img width="505" height="259" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cc8b3283-4dd0-44a7-86b3-04ecaa9fe771" />

### X (Twitter)
- **Likes:** Hides the heart button and count.
- **Comments:** Hides the reply button and sweeps away the entire comment thread below a main tweet.
- **Reposts:** Hides the retweet/repost button.

## How to use it

1. Get [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) for your browser.
2. Click the raw `fbig_like_hider.user.js` file in this repo to install it.
3. Open any of the supported sites and look for the small "G+" button in the bottom right corner.
4. Flip the toggles. It saves your settings automatically.

## Notes
Because social media sites constantly change their code under the hood, things might break randomly. If they update their layouts, this script will probably need an update too.
